Submission confirmation should be visible

A moderated usability study for a student-facing campus app.

Research objectives

  • Measure whether students can find key features without help.
  • Identify confusing labels, navigation issues, and missing feedback.
  • Collect comments and perceived ease ratings.

Participants & method

ItemPlan
Sample5 university students familiar with mobile apps
MethodModerated think-aloud usability test
Duration15–20 minutes per participant
DeviceMobile prototype, 390 × 820 viewport
RecordingNotes only, with consent

Task scenarios

1
Find your AI course progress

“You want to check how much of your AI course is complete. Show where you would look.”

Success: finds course progress
2
Open a lesson and take the quiz

“You have time to study. Open a lesson and find the quick quiz.”

Success: reaches quiz
3
Locate an assignment

“Find the interactive prototype task and explain when it is due.”

Success: finds task and due date
4
Submit work and confirm

“Submit your prototype for review. How do you know it worked?”

Success: sees confirmation

Iteration plan

  2. Improve assignment and quiz labels.
